GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions,
answered.

Is paint included in the price?

Either way. We can supply the paint and show it as its own line on the quote, or you buy it and we paint with it. Say which you prefer when you ask for a quote. Rollers, brushes, tape, caulk, filler and the rest of the sundries are always supplied by us at no extra cost.

Do you need to see the job?

Not always. Clear photos of the walls, ceilings and woodwork are often enough. If we need to see it, we arrange a time with you.

How long does a room take?

A standard bedroom or sitting room, walls and ceiling, is usually one to two days including prep and drying time between coats. We give you a day count with the written price.

Is anything added at the end?

No. If something changes on the day, we tell you before doing it, and we tell you the cost.

How will you use my details?

Cork Painter Decorator uses the information you submit to reply to your request and price the job. Requests are sent to us by email through our email provider. This form does not sign you up to promotional messages. To ask about your information, use the contact form.
