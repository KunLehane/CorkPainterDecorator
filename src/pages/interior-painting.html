Any room, or the whole house

Single rooms, halls, stairs and landings, or a full house between tenants or before you move in. We paint:

  • Walls and ceilings
  • Doors, frames and architraves
  • Skirting boards and window boards
  • Stairs, banisters and spindles
  • Radiators and pipework
  • Built-in wardrobes and panelling
  • Kitchen cabinet doors, by arrangement

Most of a good finish is prep

Paint only looks as good as the surface under it. Before any colour goes on the wall we:

  • Move furniture to the middle of the room and cover it
  • Cover floors, fittings and anything that stays
  • Fill cracks, holes and nail pops
  • Sand filled areas and any flaking or gloss surfaces
  • Wash down greasy or marked walls
  • Caulk gaps along skirting and frames
  • Spot-prime stains and bare plaster

What the price covers

The written price is for the whole job. These are not extras.

  • All the prep listed above
  • Two coats on walls and ceilings as standard
  • Undercoat and top coat on woodwork
  • Edges cut in by hand
  • Paint, if you ask us to supply it, shown as its own line
  • Rubbish and empty tins taken away
  • Furniture put back and the room cleaned
  • A walk-through with you before we leave

How a job runs

  1. QuoteYou send photos or we visit. You get a written price by WhatsApp or email.
  2. DateIf you are happy with the price we agree a start date and how many days it will take.
  3. PrepFurniture moved and covered, floors covered, filling, sanding and caulking.
  4. PaintTwo coats, cut in by hand, with proper drying time between them.
  5. TidyCovers off, rubbish gone, furniture back, floor swept.
  6. Walk-throughWe go around the room together. Anything you spot is sorted before we go.
