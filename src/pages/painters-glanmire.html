Painters And Decorators In Glanmire, Cork

Painting and decorating for homes in Glanmire.

Glanmire is north east of the city along the Glashaboy River, taking in Riverstown, Sallybrook and the estates off the old Dublin road. Most homes are family houses in estates, with some older houses along the river.

Typical jobs are full interior repaints, halls, stairs and landings, feature walls in paper, and kitchen units painted a new colour instead of replaced.

You get a written price before we start, prep included, two coats as standard, and a walk-through with you at the end.

About the area

Glanmire sits in the Glashaboy valley north east of the city, between the city and the Dublin road. With a lot of family homes in estates, painting work here is mostly interior refreshes, wallpapering and kitchen cabinet painting, with exterior repaints of render, windows and doors.

How we work

A Written Price, Proper Prep, A Tidy Finish.

Send a few photos or let us see the job. You get the price in writing before anything starts, and nothing is added at the end.

  1. 01

    Photos or a visit

    We see what the job needs

    Send photos by WhatsApp or ask us to call out. We look at the walls, ceilings and woodwork and ask any questions before we price it.

  2. 02

    You get a price

    In writing, before we start

    The price comes by WhatsApp or email. If we are supplying the paint it is shown as its own line. If something changes on the day, we tell you before doing it.

  3. 03

    We do the job

    Prep, two coats, tidy finish

    Furniture moved and covered, filling and sanding, two coats as standard. The room goes back the way we found it and we walk through it with you before we leave.
