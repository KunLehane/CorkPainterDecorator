Kitchen paint has to stick

Cabinets get handled every day and kitchens get grease in the air. Paint on a dirty or shiny surface chips. Before any finish coat goes on we:

Some foil-wrapped or damaged doors are not suitable for painting. If yours are not, we will say so at quote stage.

  • Take off doors, drawer fronts and hinges
  • Label each door and its hinges so everything goes back where it came from
  • Mask worktops, appliances, walls and floors
  • Degrease every surface to be painted
  • Lightly sand or key the surface so the primer can grip
  • Fill dents and chips
  • Prime with an adhesion primer suited to the surface: wood, MDF, or laminate and foil where suitable
  • Finish with durable coats made for cabinets and woodwork
