How we work

A written price first

We never start on a nod. You get the price in writing, by WhatsApp or email, after we have seen the job or your photos. If something changes on the day, we tell you before doing it, and we tell you the cost.

Prep is part of the job

Filling, sanding, caulking and covering are in the price. Two coats are standard. We do not count prep as an extra, because without it the finish does not last.

Tidy as we go

Floors covered, furniture covered, dust kept down, rubbish taken away. The room goes back the way it was, with the paint on the walls and nowhere else.

A walk-through before we leave

We go around the finished work with you. If you spot something, it is sorted there and then.

What we will not do

  • Start without a written price agreed
  • Skip the prep to save a day
  • Add a cost at the end that was not agreed up front
  • Leave a room for you to clean
