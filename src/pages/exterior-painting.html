The outside of the house

Front, back and gables, or just the bit that faces the road. We paint:

Glass is masked before the frames are painted, so the line against the window stays straight.

  • Masonry, render and pebble dash
  • Fascia, soffit and bargeboards
  • Front and back doors
  • Window frames and sills
  • Garden walls, gates and railings
  • Sheds and garage doors

Clean, dry and sound before it is painted

Outside paint fails when it goes onto dirt, damp or loose material. Before the first coat we:

Flaking sills and scuffed render like this are scraped, filled and primed before any top coat goes on.

  • Power wash or scrub the walls and let them dry
  • Treat algae and mould where it is present
  • Scrape and sand loose or flaking paint
  • Fill cracks and patch small areas of damaged render
  • Prime bare patches and sound rust on metalwork
  • Mask windows, doors, lights and paths
  • Check the weather forecast before starting masonry coats

What the price covers

The written price is for the whole job. These are not extras.

  • All the prep listed above
  • Two coats of masonry paint as standard
  • Undercoat and gloss or satin on woodwork and metal
  • Cutting in around windows, doors and trims
  • Ladders or access equipment for a standard two-storey house
  • Paint, if you ask us to supply it, shown as its own line
  • Paths, drives and gardens left clean
  • A walk-around with you before we leave

Scaffolding or a tower, if a job needs it, is priced separately and shown on the quote before you agree to anything.

How a job runs

  1. QuoteYou send photos of each side of the house, or we call out. You get a written price.
  2. DateWe agree a start date and how many dry days we need. Exterior dates move with the weather.
  3. PrepWash, dry, scrape, fill, prime and mask.
  4. PaintTwo coats on masonry, undercoat and top coat on woodwork and metal.
  5. TidyMasking off, paths and drive swept and washed, rubbish gone.
  6. Walk-aroundWe go around the outside with you. Anything you spot is sorted before we go.
