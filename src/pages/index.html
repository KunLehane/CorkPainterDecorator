What a job includes

Every quote covers the full job, not just the painting. Nothing on this list is an extra.

  • A written price before we start
  • Furniture moved to the centre and covered
  • Floors and fittings covered
  • Cracks and holes filled
  • Surfaces sanded and dusted down
  • Edges cut in by hand
  • Two coats as standard
  • Woodwork undercoated where it needs it
  • Rubbish taken away
  • Room put back the way we found it
  • A walk-through with you at the end
  • Touch-ups before we leave, not after you ring

Services

What we paint

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, doors, skirting, stairs and halls. Single rooms or the whole house.

Exterior painting

Masonry and render, fascia and soffit, front doors, windows and gates.

Wallpapering

Feature walls and full rooms. Old paper stripped, walls lined, patterns matched.
