Painting And Decorating Across Cork

A Finish That Lasts Starts With The Prep

Paint only looks as good as the surface under it. That is why prep is in every price: filling, sanding, caulking, priming where it is needed, and covering everything that stays in the room.

What a job includes

Every quote covers the whole job. Nothing on this list is an extra.
  • Furniture and floors covered. Furniture goes to the middle of the room and is covered, and so are the floors and fittings.
  • Cracks and holes filled. Filled areas are sanded and dusted down before any paint goes on.
  • Edges cut in by hand. Straight lines where walls meet ceilings, skirting and frames.
  • Woodwork undercoated. Undercoat where the woodwork needs it, then the top coat.
  • A walk-through at the end. We go around the finished work with you, and touch-ups are done before we leave.

Whether it is one room this month or the whole house in stages, you get a written price first and a room left tidy.
