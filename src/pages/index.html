What we paint

  • Interior paintingWalls, ceilings, doors, skirting, stairs and halls. Single rooms or the whole house.
  • Exterior paintingMasonry and render, fascia and soffit, front doors, windows and gates.
  • WallpaperingFeature walls and full rooms. Old paper stripped, walls lined, patterns matched.
  • Kitchen cabinet paintingCabinet doors, drawer fronts, islands and built-in units, painted or sprayed.
  • Commercial paintingOffices, shops, cafes, rentals and apartment common areas. Out of hours by arrangement.
