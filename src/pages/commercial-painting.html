Prep that works around a business

The same prep as any job, planned so you can keep trading where possible. Before and during the work we:

  • Agree working hours and areas with you before we start
  • Protect stock, fittings, furniture and equipment
  • Cover floors and keep walkways clear
  • Work in sections so parts of the premises stay in use
  • Use low-odour paints where staff or customers are nearby
  • Fill, sand and spot-prime walls and woodwork
  • Clear up at the end of each shift

What the price covers

The written price is for the whole job. These are not extras.

  • All the prep listed above
  • Two coats on walls and ceilings as standard
  • Undercoat and top coat on woodwork
  • A method statement if your business or building needs one
  • Paint, if you ask us to supply it, shown as its own line
  • Rubbish and empty tins taken away
  • A tidy site at the end of each day
  • A walk-through with you before we leave

Out-of-hours work, access equipment and anything specific to the building are shown on the quote before you agree to anything.

How a job runs

  1. QuoteSend photos and your opening hours, or we call out. You get a written price.
  2. PlanWe agree dates, working hours and which areas go first. A method statement if you need one.
  3. PrepStock and fittings protected, floors covered, filling, sanding and priming.
  4. PaintTwo coats, one section at a time, with the site left tidy at the end of each shift.
  5. TidyCovers off, rubbish gone, fittings back in place.
  6. Walk-through and invoiceWe go around the premises with you, sort anything you spot, then send a written invoice.
